# HURS Manual for Data Entry (2024)

> Source: `REF.pdf` in this repository.

## Overview
The Healthy University Rating System (HURS) is a tool used to track the progress and success of health promotion universities. It is based on the AUN Healthy University Framework (HUF) and combines the QS Star Rating System and the UI GreenMetric scoring approach. The AUN-HPN Secretariat Office sends completed questionnaires to reviewers, who evaluate submitted evidence and assign the final rating and stars. 

## Qualifications for HURS Data Entry Personnel
- Must understand the Healthy University Framework (HUF).
- Must understand the criteria used in the Healthy University Rating System (HURS).

## Responsibilities of HURS Data Entry Personnel
- Collect and submit evidence from the past year that matches the questions and criteria in the HURS assessment.
- Submit the evidence through the online HURS questionnaire for university review and approval.

## Steps in the HURS Data Entry Workflow

### 1. Log in to the system
1. Open a browser and navigate to: `https://hurs.mahidol.ac.th/`.
2. On the main screen, select **Start** to begin the assessment process (see Figure 1-1 in the PDF).
3. Sign in with your username and password to access the system (see Figure 1-2).
4. After sign-in, the system will display the landing page for the assessment (see Figure 1-3).

### 2. Start the assessment
1. The system opens with an **Introduction** page.
2. Select the **Start** button to open the assessment sections (see Figure 2-1).
3. The system displays 5 assessment sections (see Figure 2-2).

### 3. Submit the assessment
1. After completing the required sections, submit the answers (see Figure 2-3).
2. The system asks for confirmation before final submission (see Figure 2-4).
3. Once submitted, the assessment is sent to the university approver and the status is displayed (see Figure 2-5).
